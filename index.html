What it is

Plain files, in your folder

Markdown on disk, in a folder you pick — iCloud Drive or anywhere else. Readable without Notesage, and still readable in ten years.

A reading pile that keeps track

Share an article to Notesage and it is saved as a file. The app remembers how far you read, and can read it aloud.

Nothing phones home

No account, no analytics, no server of ours. The app talks to the network when you ask it to fetch something, and not otherwise.

The same library everywhere

The Mac app and the iPhone app read the same folder. Pins, reading progress and folder colours travel as files beside your notes.
